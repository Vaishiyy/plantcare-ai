# Surface Brief: PlantCare AI Mobile-First App

<!-- impeccable:surface-brief-schema 1 -->

## Scope & Visitor Mode
- Surface: All 7 interactive screens of PlantCare AI (Home Dashboard, Plant Details, Live Monitoring, AI Health Analysis, History & Analytics, Alerts & Notifications, Settings & Device Connection).
- Mode: Operate (Visitor monitors, inspects sensor telemetry, analyzes ML health classifications, configures thresholds/alerts, and connects STM32 hardware or runs realistic demo simulations).

## Target Audience & Task
- Audience: College evaluators, engineering students, embedded systems faculty, and plant owners.
- Key Task: Verify real-time soil moisture, temperature, humidity, and light readings from STM32 over Bluetooth (UART/BLE), observe ML classification output ("Healthy", "Needs Water", "Too Dry", "Poor Environment"), diagnose conditions with clear environmental context, and export analytical history.

## Direction contract

THESIS: An organic, calm botanical companion that presents authentic embedded hardware telemetry with the warmth and elegance of modern consumer plant-care apps, refusing both generic gray IoT dashboards and cartoonish toy interfaces.

OWN-WORLD: Soft porcelain cream background (#F9F8F5 / #F4F1EA), deep evergreen forest primary (#1B4332, #2D6A4F), soothing sage tint cards (#EBF5EE, #D8F3DC), sun-warmed amber (#E9C46A) and terra-cotta coral (#E76F51) for critical alerts. Clean organic curves (16-24px radii), tactile elevation shadows, and lucid SVG instrumentation.

STORY: The user feels an immediate emotional bond with their monitored plant while having instant access to scientific telemetry, clear explanations of what the microcontroller's ML model detected, and actionable next steps without fake diagnostic overclaiming.

FIRST VIEWPORT: In mobile frame (and centered responsive card on desktop): warm greeting with plant parent avatar, hardware sync & Bluetooth status badge, prominent plant condition overview card with dynamic health status indicator, followed by four dedicated tactile sensor telemetry cards (Moisture, Temp, Humidity, Light) with real-time trendlines.
